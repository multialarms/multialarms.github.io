Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 6, 2026

MultiAlarms ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website multialarms.com (the "Site"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from you when you visit our Site, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

Since our Site is completely free and does not require user accounts, logins, registrations, or any form of personal submission, we collect very limited personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our Site, we (and any future third-party services we may integrate) may automatically collect certain non-personal or aggregated information, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Device type
  • Pages you visit on our Site
  • Time and date of your visit
  • Time spent on pages
  • Referring website (if any)

This data helps us understand how visitors use the Site, improve functionality, and (in the future) support advertising if we implement it.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies (small text files stored on your device) or similar technologies for basic site functionality, such as remembering preferences or analyzing traffic. Currently, we use minimal or no cookies beyond what may be required for core browsing.

If we add analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) or advertising (e.g., Google AdSense) in the future, third parties may place cookies or use technologies like web beacons to collect data for analytics or personalized ads. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

2. How We Use the Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

  • Operate and maintain the Site
  • Analyze usage trends and improve user experience
  • Detect and prevent technical issues or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Current and Future)

Currently

We do not display advertisements or use third-party tracking for monetization.

Future Plans

We are working toward approval for Google AdSense to display ads. If implemented:

  • Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Site or other websites.
  • These cookies enable interest-based (personalized) advertising.
  • Third parties may use web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to collect information as a result of ad serving on our Site.

To learn more about how Google uses data when you visit partner sites, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

If we integrate other tools (e.g., Google Analytics), similar disclosures would apply.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

  • As required by law (e.g., court order or subpoena)

Aggregated, non-personal data may be shared for analytics purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any collected data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can clear cookies and site data via your browser.

You can opt out of interest-based ads as noted above.

Depending on your location (e.g., under CCPA/CPRA in California, GDPR in the EU/EEA, or VCDPA in Virginia), you may have rights to access, delete, or opt out of certain data processing. Contact us if applicable (see below).

Since we collect minimal personal data, many of these rights may not apply in practice.
